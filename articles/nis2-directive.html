NIS2: Europe's New Cybersecurity Framework

July 27, 202612 min
Quick Summary Read ▼

The NIS2 Directive is the EU's updated cybersecurity framework, replacing the original 2016 NIS Directive. It expands the scope from 7 to 15 sectors (including healthcare, manufacturing, and public administration), introduces a tiered system of 'essential' and 'important' entities, and sets strict incident reporting timelines (24 hours for early warning). Penalties can reach up to 10 million EUR or 2% of global turnover. This article explains who is affected, what compliance requires, and how organisations can prepare.

#NIS2 #Cybersecurity #CriticalInfrastructure

The NIS2 Directive modernises EU cybersecurity requirements for critical infrastructure, with stricter obligations, broader scope, and heavier penalties for non-compliance.
