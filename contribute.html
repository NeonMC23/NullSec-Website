Contribute

NullSec is built by the community. Everyone can help, regardless of their skills.

✏️

Writing Guides

Help others by writing clear, practical guides. No technical writing experience needed — we help you structure and edit.

🌐

Translation

Translate articles and guides into your language. We are building toward full EN/FR support and beyond.

💻

Programming

Contribute to our open source projects on GitHub. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and Linux scripting.

🎨

Design

Help improve the website, create illustrations, or design icons and diagrams for our guides.

🔍

Testing

Test guides and tools, report bugs, suggest improvements. Your feedback makes everything better.

📖

Documentation

Improve our documentation, write READMEs, create cheatsheets, and organize our knowledge base.

🔌

Research

Investigate privacy tools, test software, compare alternatives, and share your findings with the community.

🐛

Bug Reports

Found a broken link, typo, or issue? Report it on GitHub or Discord. Every fix helps.

Ready to Help?

Join our Discord and introduce yourself in the #contributors channel.
